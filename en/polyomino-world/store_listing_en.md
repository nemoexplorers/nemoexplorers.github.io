## Full description

Discover a world built from squares!

Nemo Explorers: Polyomino is a child-friendly puzzle game where young explorers
can build, find, observe, and fit together shapes made from connected squares.
The focus is on trying different ideas and discovering new shapes at a comfortable
pace.

POLYOMINO LAB

Build and discover 21 polyominoes, from monominoes to pentominoes, on a 5×5 board.

POLYOMINO BOOK

Review every shape you have discovered. Rotate and flip each polyomino to explore
how its appearance changes.

FIND POLYOMINOES

Search a changing 10×10 board for hidden polyominoes and practice careful visual
observation.

WORLD ADVENTURE

Complete target boards across 4 worlds and 32 stages. Arrange the pieces, collect
stars, and unlock the next challenge.

DAILY PUZZLE

Try a new pentomino puzzle each day. Use the calendar to review completed days and
your best times.

TREASURE QUEST

Place blocks on an 8×8 board, clear horizontal or vertical lines, and collect
treasure while improving your score and combo.

FILLOMINO ADVENTURE

Complete number-based regions on a 6×8 puzzle board. Make every region match its
number without joining equal-sized regions together.

MADE FOR YOUNG EXPLORERS

• Simple taps and drag-and-drop controls
• Friendly guides for each activity
• Phone and tablet layouts
• No account or sign-in required
• Core games available offline
• Progress saved on the device
• Guardian Area for settings and progress management

Explore, experiment, and build your own path through the world of polyominoes.
