## Full description

Welcome to a colorful world of connected cubes!

Nemo Explorers: Soma Cube is a child-friendly 3D puzzle game where young
explorers connect cubes, discover new shapes, and solve puzzles at their own
pace. Rotate the board, try different ideas, and enjoy learning through play.

SOMA CUBE LAB

Join cubes on a 3×3×3 board and discover all seven Soma cubes. Build each shape
yourself, then try again whenever you like.

SOMA CUBE BOOK

Review every Soma cube you have discovered. Move between shapes and rotate them
from different angles to explore their structure.

WORLD ADVENTURE

Complete target boards across 4 worlds and 32 stages. Arrange the given Soma
cubes, fill the empty spaces, collect stars, and unlock new challenges.

DAILY PUZZLE

Try a new 3×3×3 puzzle every day using seven Soma cubes. Check the calendar to
review your completed days and personal records.

TREASURE QUEST

Place blocks on an 8×8 board and clear horizontal or vertical lines. Build
combos, improve your score, and collect treasure as you play.

MADE FOR YOUNG EXPLORERS

- Simple taps and drag-and-drop controls
- Friendly guides for each activity
- Phone and tablet layouts
- No account or sign-in required
- Core games available offline
- Progress saved on the device
- Guardian Area for settings and progress management

Connect, discover, and solve your way through the world of Soma cubes.
