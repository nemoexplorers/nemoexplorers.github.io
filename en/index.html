ABOUT NEMO EXPLORERS

Discover the joy of thinking

Nemo Explorers is a playful learning game series that invites children to explore shapes, space, language, and numbers through hands-on discovery.

We value the experience of trying ideas and finding new approaches over reaching an answer quickly. Each game lets children explore at their own pace while building observation, reasoning, and problem-solving skills.
