## Full description

Explore Number World with Nemo!

Nemo Explorers: Number World is a learning game for children to explore numbers and quantities through hands-on activities and puzzles.

Children can count, compare, split groups, and discover patterns at their own pace. There are no time limits or harsh failure messages.

■ Number Lab
 - Try five activities: Meet the Numbers, Count the Fruit, Find the Missing Number, Compare Numbers, and Split into Groups. Explore how numbers look, what they represent, their order and size, and how quantities can be split.

■ Number Maze
 - Start at 1 and connect the numbers in order to make a path to 36. Tap or drag to practice number order.

■ Number Pairs
 - Find two numbers that add up to the target. Discover different combinations as you play.

■ Number Patterns
 - Look at a sequence and find the missing number. Explore patterns made by adding, subtracting, and repeating changes.

■ Number Balloon
 - Solve a question and pop the balloon with the correct answer. Practice numbers before and after, addition, subtraction, and comparison.

■ Futoshiki
 - Follow the greater than and less than signs while placing 1 to 5 once in each row and column.

■ Sudoku
 - Place 1 to 9 once in each row, column, and 3×3 box. Work through easy, medium, and hard puzzles.

■ Made for children
 - Large buttons and simple touch controls
 - Step-by-step guides with Nemo, a friendly blue square mascot
 - Portrait layouts for phones and tablets
 - No sign-in or account required
 - Core learning activities and games work offline
 - Separate progress for each profile and a Parent Area for settings

Count, explore, and solve puzzles with Nemo at your own pace.
